How it works

  • Team similarity compares your starting XI, bench and captain with each creator's, gameweek by gameweek.
  • Transfer similarity counts transfers you made in the same gameweek as the creator, excluding wildcard and free hit weeks.
  • Copycat similarity counts only those shared transfers you made after the creator did.

Creator squads come from the FPLGameweek content creator league and are refreshed daily.
